Good to know

Will my order arrive before Christmas?

Order by December 5 for estimated US Christmas delivery. Later orders may still arrive in time, but we can't guarantee it.

When will my order arrive?

US orders usually arrive in 9–17 days; Canada, the UK and Australia in 10–20 days; most other countries in 12–25 days. You get a tracking link by email as soon as it ships.

Will I pay customs fees or taxes?

No. Our prices include all taxes and import duties, so the price at checkout is the final price. If a carrier ever asks you for a fee, contact us before paying and we'll take care of it.

What if something arrives damaged or doesn't fit?

Contact support@suzeno.com within 30 days of delivery. If it arrives damaged or faulty, we'll refund you or send a replacement. For a different size, email us and we'll help.

Do bundles save money?

Yes. Most products have a bundle discount when you buy 2 or more. You'll see the bundle price on each product page, and the discount is applied automatically in your cart.
