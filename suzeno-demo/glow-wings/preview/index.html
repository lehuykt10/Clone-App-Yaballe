Description

A small light that wraps around your dog's collar and projects glowing wings onto the path around them, with a side light that helps others spot your dog after dark.

How it looks in real life

The light projects the wing pattern onto the ground beside and behind your dog. It looks brightest on dark, smooth surfaces like paths, pavement and snow, and softer on grass or under bright street lights.

Product photos are illustrations of the effect. The wings appear on the ground, not attached to your dog.

What's in the box
  • 1 Glow Wings light with adjustable strap, in the wing style you choose
  • 1 USB charging cable
Battery & charging

Built-in rechargeable battery. Charge it with the included USB cable from any USB charger or laptop. One charge covers several evening walks; when the light starts to dim, recharge it.

Fit, care & safety
  • Wrap the strap around your dog's collar or harness and pull it snug so the light faces backwards.
  • Not a toy. Don't let your dog chew the light.
  • Handles light rain and snow. Don't submerge it or let your dog swim with it.
  • Wipe clean with a dry cloth.
  • Always walk on a leash and stay visible yourself. The light is an extra, not a replacement for reflective gear.
