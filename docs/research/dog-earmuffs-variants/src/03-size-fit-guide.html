STANDARD cut 标准版Oval face opening. For normal muzzle length, prick or floppy ears. Floppy ears fold down inside the pod pocket (pocket depth +1.5 cm).标准椭圆开口
WIDE-FACE cut 宽脸版 (brachycephalic)Wider, shorter face opening so it never presses on eyes or nose folds; lower front edge. For Frenchie, Pug, Boxer, Shih Tzu.短鼻犬专用宽开口，不压眼睛与鼻褶
How to measure 测量方法: Head = around the widest part of the skull, in front of the ears, under the jaw. Neck = where a collar sits. Between two sizes → choose the larger one.
