**Hot or cold — your call**

**Made for days like these**
- Long screen days that end with a heavy head

- Quiet, dark naps — anywhere
- Puffy morning eyes
- The morning after the party

**How to use**
1. Chill or warm the cap.
2. Slip it on — pull down for darkness, flip up to see.
3. Relax for 15–20 minutes. Re-chill and repeat as needed.

**FAQ**
- *How long does it stay cold?* — About 15–20 minutes per session; re-chill between uses. *(đo thật trên mẫu)*
- *Can I sleep in it?* — It's designed for short relaxation sessions. Remove it before falling asleep for long periods.
- *Is it too cold straight from the freezer?* — If it feels too cold, let it rest a minute or wear a thin cloth underneath. The fridge gives a gentler chill.
- *How do I clean it?* — Wipe the surface with a damp cloth; don't machine-wash the gel. *(xác nhận)*
