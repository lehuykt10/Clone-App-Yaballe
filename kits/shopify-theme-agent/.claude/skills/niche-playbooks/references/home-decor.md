# Home decor (decor, lighting, bedding, organisation)

**Buyer:** wants the room to *look like the photo*; fears wrong size, cheap material,
colour different from photos, hard assembly.

## Product page must-haves
1. Styled room photos + plain product photos + **scale reference** (person, sofa, hand)
2. Dimensions tab with a simple dimension drawing; weight; materials
3. Assembly / installation steps (how_it_works) and what's included
4. Colour accuracy note; real customer room photos when available
5. For lighting: bulb type, brightness (lumens), plug type per country, power adapter
6. For bedding/textiles: size chart (Twin/Full/Queen/King), fabric, washing

## Offers that work
- Sets (pair of lamps, 3 frames), "buy 2 save 10 %"
- Free shipping; 30-day returns (large items: clarify return shipping cost)

## brand.json hints
Mood "Calm & natural" · radius `rounded` or `soft` · trust icons `truck`, `return`, `star` ·
tabs: Dimensions / Materials & care / What's included / Shipping / Returns.

## Red flags
- Electrical items: plug type and voltage must match destination; UL/CE certification for claims
- Candles/diffusers: fire safety warnings; essential oil health claims are not allowed
- "Solid wood" vs MDF/veneer must be accurate
- Photos taken from the competitor's rooms or Pinterest → copyright
