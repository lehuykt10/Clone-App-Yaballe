# Fitness & health (gear, posture, massage, wellness)

**Buyer:** motivated but sceptical; has tried things before; fears it won't work for them
or will hurt.

## Product page must-haves
1. Demo of correct use (video), who it's for / not for
2. Honest expectations: "use 10 min a day", no guaranteed outcomes
3. Specs, sizes (size guide for braces, belts), materials, adjustability
4. Safety warnings: consult a doctor if…, not for pregnancy/injuries (as relevant)
5. Routine / how-to-use steps; downloadable guide (original)

## Offers that work
- 1 / 2 (partner) / 3; bundles with accessories (bands + bag)
- Guarantee framed as "try it for 30 days"

## brand.json hints
Mood "Bold & energetic" or "Fresh & clean" · trust icons `stopwatch`, `truck`, `heart` ·
tabs: How to use / Size & fit / Specs / Safety / Shipping.

## Red flags (strictest niche on ads)
- Medical claims: "cures back pain", "fixes posture permanently", "treats sciatica", "lose 10 kg" → banned (FTC, Meta, Google). Use "supports", "reminds you to…", "helps you feel…" carefully
- Before/after body images and weight-loss claims → Meta prohibits
- Medical devices (TENS, massagers with therapeutic claims) may need FDA clearance
- Supplements: not covered by this kit; heavy regulation
