# Gadgets & electronics (phone accessories, small devices)

**Buyer:** problem-solution buyer from a video ad; fears it's a cheap knock-off, won't be
compatible, breaks in a month, no support.

## Product page must-haves
1. Hero demo video solving the problem; GIF-style loops for each feature
2. **Compatibility** list (phone models, ports, voltages) right under the title or in a tab
3. Specs table (battery mAh, charging time, dimensions, weight, materials)
4. What's in the box; warranty length and how to claim
5. Comparison vs the category ("regular chargers") with honest rows
6. Troubleshooting FAQ (pairing, charging, reset)

## Offers that work
- 1 / 2 (save 10 %) / 3 (family pack, save 20 %)
- Extended warranty only if you really honour it
- Accessories as add-ons (cables, cases)

## brand.json hints
Mood "Tech & precise" or "Bold & energetic" · radius `soft` · trust icons `lightning_bolt`,
`truck`, `lock` · tabs: Specs / Compatibility / What's in the box / Warranty / Shipping.

## Red flags
- Batteries: lithium shipping restrictions (some countries/air routes); check supplier line
- FCC/CE/UKCA marking needed to sell in US/EU/UK; "military grade", "MagSafe" (trademark) without licence
- Health claims (EMF protection, blue-light cures) are not allowed
- Bluetooth trackers, cameras, spy devices: privacy law issues, ad bans
