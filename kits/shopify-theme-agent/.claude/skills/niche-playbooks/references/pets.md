# Pets (accessories, keepsakes, apparel, grooming, toys)

**Buyer:** pet parents 25–55, mostly women; buy with emotion ("my baby"), gift for other
pet owners, memorial buyers (grief, need gentle tone).
**Main fears:** Is it safe for my pet? Will it fit? Will my pet tolerate it?

## Product page must-haves
1. Photos/video of **real pets using it**, several breeds/sizes (dog + cat if both fit)
2. Size guide by **weight and chest/neck** (`size_guide`) for anything worn
3. Safety: materials, no small parts, washing; FAQ "Is it safe for my pet?"
4. How it works (3–4 steps) for anything DIY or with a learning curve
5. Comparison vs a *category* ("regular carriers", "ink pad kits")
6. Gentle memorial block if the product can be a keepsake
7. UGC gallery once customers send photos (with permission)

## Offers that work
- Bundle "1 / 2 (one to keep, one to gift) / 3 (multi-pet homes)", tier 2 pre-selected
- Free shipping; 30-day guarantee
- Add-ons: name tag, extra refills, gift box (only if really stocked)

## brand.json hints
Mood "Warm & caring" · radius `rounded` · trust icons `paw_print`, `truck`, `heart` ·
tabs: What's in the box / Size & fit / Care / Shipping / Guarantee.

## FAQ topics
Safety · sizing for my breed · pet won't stay still · washing · cats vs dogs · shipping · damaged item.

## Red flags (claims and ads)
- "Non-toxic", "vet approved", "calming", "anxiety relief" → need proof (certificate, vet statement)
- Health claims on supplements, calming products, flea/tick → regulated (EPA/FDA); avoid unless licensed
- Meta ads: no before/after of sick animals; memorial ads must not be exploitative
- Photos of pets in unsafe situations (car seats without restraint etc.) get rejected
