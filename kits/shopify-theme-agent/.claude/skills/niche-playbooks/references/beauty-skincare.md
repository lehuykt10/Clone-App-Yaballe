# Beauty & skincare (skincare, cosmetics, hair, beauty devices)

**Buyer:** 18–45, researches ingredients and reviews, fears irritation and wasted money.
**Main fears:** Will it work on my skin type? Is it safe? How long until results?

## Product page must-haves
1. **Ingredients / materials** tab (full INCI list for cosmetics) and "Suitable for" skin types
2. How to use (steps) + how often + when to expect results (honest ranges)
3. Texture/application video; real-customer photos only (no retouched before/after)
4. Patch-test note and warnings
5. FAQ: sensitive skin, pregnancy, combining with other products, shelf life after opening
6. For devices: specs, charging, warranty, safety certifications

## Offers that work
- Bundles: 1 / 2 (save 10–15 %) / 3 (a 3-month routine); or "routine set" (cleanser + serum + cream)
- Subscribe-and-save only with a subscription app
- Money-back guarantee (define: even if opened?)

## brand.json hints
Mood "Fresh & clean" or "Soft luxury" · radius `soft` · trust icons `leaf`, `truck`, `heart` ·
tabs: Ingredients / How to use / Shipping / Guarantee.

## Red flags
- Drug claims are illegal for cosmetics: "treats acne", "cures", "anti-inflammatory", "heals",
  "regrows hair", "removes wrinkles permanently", "boosts collagen" (FDA/FTC, EU, Meta, Google)
- "Clinically proven", "dermatologist tested/recommended", "hypoallergenic", "organic",
  "cruelty-free", "vegan" → only with documents/certification
- Before/after images: Meta restricts them; never edited
- Devices (LED, microcurrent, IPL): may need FDA clearance for medical claims; say "cosmetic use"
- Labeling: US cosmetics need ingredient list + net quantity; EU needs a Responsible Person
