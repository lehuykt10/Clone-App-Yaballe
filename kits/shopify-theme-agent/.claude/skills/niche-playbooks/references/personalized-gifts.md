# Personalized products & gifts

**Buyer:** buying for someone else, often against a date (birthday, holiday, memorial);
fears typos, ugly preview, late delivery.

## Product page must-haves
1. Personalisation fields on the product page (name, date, photo upload) → needs an app
   (e.g. a product options/customizer app) or Shopify line item properties
2. **Preview** or clear examples of finished results; font choices shown
3. Production time + shipping time shown separately ("Made in 2–4 days, delivered in …")
4. Order-by dates for holidays in the announcement bar (only real cut-offs)
5. Proof approval process explained (if offered)
6. Gift options: box, message card, send to recipient

## Offers that work
- 2nd item 20 % off (family members), matching sets (couple, siblings)
- Rush production upgrade (only if the supplier really does it)

## brand.json hints
Mood "Warm & caring" · trust icons `heart`, `truck`, `star` · delivery `note` mentions
production time · tabs: Personalisation / Production & shipping / Size / Care / Returns
(custom items usually non-returnable unless defective: say so clearly).

## Red flags
- Customers uploading copyrighted images (characters, logos, celebrity photos) → add a
  content rule in Terms
- Memorial products: gentle wording, no fear-based ads
- Promising a delivery date the supplier can't meet → chargebacks
