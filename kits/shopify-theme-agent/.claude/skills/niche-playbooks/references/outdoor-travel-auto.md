# Outdoor, travel & car accessories

**Buyer:** practical, reads specs, fears it won't fit their car/bag or won't survive real use.

## Product page must-haves
1. Fit/compatibility: car make/model list or universal fit dimensions; luggage airline sizes
2. Durability proof: materials, weight capacity, water resistance rating (IPX), load tests
3. In-use photos outdoors / in the car; installation video
4. Specs table; what's in the box; warranty
5. FAQ: installation time, will it fit, cleaning, cold/heat

## Offers that work
- Front + rear sets, 2 for family cars, travel kits (organiser + pouch)
- Free shipping; 30-day fit guarantee (if you accept returns)

## brand.json hints
Mood "Bold & energetic" or "Tech & precise" · trust icons `truck`, `check_mark`, `return` ·
tabs: Fit & compatibility / Specs / Installation / Shipping / Returns.

## Red flags
- Car safety items (child seats, airbags, brake parts, tire tools) → strict regulation (FMVSS/ECE), avoid
- Windshield-mounted items: legality varies by country/state
- "Waterproof" vs "water resistant" must match the IP rating
- Car brand logos (Tesla, BMW) on products → trademark
