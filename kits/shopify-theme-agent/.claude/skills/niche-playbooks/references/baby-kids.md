# Baby & kids (baby products, toys, kids' clothing)

**Buyer:** parents and grandparents; safety is the #1 decision factor, then ease of use.

## Product page must-haves
1. **Age range** clearly stated (e.g. "3+ years"), choking-hazard warning when relevant
2. Safety & materials tab: certifications (CPC for US kids' products, EN71 / CE for EU, ASTM F963 for toys)
3. Real photos with children only with parent consent; otherwise product-only/hands
4. Washing/cleaning; dimensions; what's included
5. FAQ: safe for newborns? washable? batteries?

## Offers that work
- Gift bundles (birthday, baby shower), "buy 2 for twins/siblings"
- Gift wrap / message (if real)

## brand.json hints
Mood "Playful" or "Warm & caring" · radius `rounded` · trust icons `heart`, `truck`,
`check_mark` · tabs: Age & safety / Materials & care / Size / Shipping / Returns.

## Red flags (legal minimum, not optional)
- US: children's products (≤12 years) need a Children's Product Certificate (CPSIA) and tracking labels; toys need ASTM F963 testing
- EU: toys need CE + EN71; UK: UKCA
- Sleep products (positioners, inclined sleepers, weighted sleep sacks) are banned or restricted in the US
- Teething products with amber/beads: safety warnings, often banned in ads
- No "medical", "safe sleep", "prevents SIDS" claims
