# Kitchen (tools, coffee & tea, food storage)

**Buyer:** home cooks and gifters; fears it's gimmicky, hard to clean, not food-safe.

## Product page must-haves
1. Demo video: the product doing the job in under 15 seconds
2. Materials: food-grade material names (304 stainless, borosilicate glass, BPA-free plastic)
3. Cleaning: dishwasher safe? parts that come apart?
4. Capacity/size with a scale photo; compatibility (induction, cup sizes)
5. How it works steps; recipe/tips content (great for SEO and email)

## Offers that work
- Bundles 1/2/3 ("one for home, one for the office, one gift")
- Starter kit with consumables (filters, refills) if really stocked
- Recipe e-book as a free bonus (original content)

## brand.json hints
Mood "Calm & natural" or "Fresh & clean" · trust icons `serving_dish`, `truck`, `heart` ·
tabs: What's in the box / Materials & care / Size / Shipping / Guarantee.

## Red flags
- "Food safe", "BPA-free", "FDA approved" (FDA doesn't "approve" kitchenware) → use "made with food-grade …" only with supplier test reports (LFGB/FDA material tests)
- Health claims ("detox", "alkaline water cures") are not allowed
- Knives: some ad platforms restrict weapon-like imagery
